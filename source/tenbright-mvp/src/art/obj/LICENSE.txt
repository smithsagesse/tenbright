Story-object pictures in this folder

Most pictures are from Microsoft Fluent Emoji (GitHub project microsoft/fluentui-emoji),
taken from the @iconify-json/fluent-emoji package, version 1.2.7, and used under the MIT License below.
These were drawn for Tenbright in the same style: eraser, bead, marble, stamp, acorn, marker, piggy-bank.
